Verify a mix design approval

Drop in a KYTC approval PDF. This checks the signature on it — whether the design has been altered since it was approved, and who approved it.

Choose an approval PDF or drag it here
What this proves. That the design inside this file is exactly what KYTC signed, under that approval number, by that reviewer, on that date. Change any value in the design and it stops verifying.

What it does not prove. That the approval is still current. KYTC may have superseded or withdrawn it since — this page has no way to know, because nothing is stored. For anything that matters, confirm with Central Office.
